Northwind Studio is a static-first website template for small businesses that want a distinct public site and a low-overhead editing workflow.

This homepage is now just a normal page in the CMS. That means the business owner can rewrite it, re-theme it, replace its imagery, or even choose a different page to become the site home without touching the codebase.

Use the collections for journals, galleries, or events, and use standalone pages when content should sit outside those grouped streams.
