Northwind Studio is a demonstration business for this template. The page is intentionally written in a tone that could be adapted to an artist, a florist, a gardener, or a service-based local business.

Because the site is built from content files, owners can replace this copy directly from the admin area without touching the codebase. The same page renderer is reused for public visitors and the in-browser admin preview, so what you see before publishing is what gets shipped.

This section is a good place to explain your point of view, your process, and why your work feels different from generic competitors.
